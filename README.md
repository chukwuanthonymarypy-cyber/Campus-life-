# Campus Life

A fictional, browser-based 3D campus-life game. Walk behind your student through Sunrise University, its school gate and two outside lodge streets. Explore lectures, shops, music, student services, traders, traffic, moving students and a football pitch with animated pickup play. The standalone `index.html` works offline for the solo game and scripted NPC chat. **Live guest chat and shared activities require the included Node server and at least one other visitor.**

## Play

```bash
npm install
npm run build
npm start
```

Open `http://localhost:8000` in a browser. Open it in **two separate browser profiles or private windows** to try shared activities. Each participant should choose a different guest name. Both must connect to the same server.

- **Modern welcome template:** a dedicated responsive glass-and-gradient welcome screen opens with **“Welcome to Campus Life”**, a short campus-life introduction, embedded campus artwork, name and course selection, and **“Start your campus journey”**. The button starts JAMB preparation; it does not bypass the mandatory mini-exam. The artwork is embedded in the standalone HTML, so the welcome screen works offline. The rest of the game UI is unchanged by this redesign.
- **Mandatory admission:** name your student and choose a course, select a JAMB preparation plan, answer the five-question fictional exam, and see your score. If below the course target, prepare and retake as often as needed; campus is inaccessible until you pass. After accepting admission, a one-time fictional ₦10,000 bursary helps you choose a hostel or lodge. Renting immediately is optional: explore first if you want, but a paid room is needed to sleep. Existing saves that bypassed JAMB must complete it on their next load; their campus progress is retained.
- **Full-window play:** the 3D scene fills the browser on desktop and mobile. Balance, hunger, energy and in-game time remain at the top; the map and chat float over the world. On desktop and mobile, tap the **🏛 Campus** side tab directly below **💬 Chat** to open one menu for daily details, housing, money, bag, timetable, music and other actions. Opening one tab closes the other. The old **New game** button is removed; existing browser saves are retained. Tap **⛶ Fullscreen** to hide browser chrome where supported (press Escape to exit). Browser or embedded previews may block the Fullscreen API, but the game still fills the window.
- **Course-based school fees:** every 20-school-day semester has a fictional fee around ₦50,000, varying by course: Computer Science ₦50,000; Medicine ₦65,000; Mass Communication ₦44,000; Business Administration ₦48,000; Law ₦56,000; Nursing ₦55,000; Pharmacy ₦60,000; Civil Engineering ₦54,000; Accounting ₦50,000; Economics ₦46,000; Architecture ₦58,000; Education ₦42,000. After passing JAMB, request **parent payment** at admission or later from **🏛 Campus → 🎓 School fees** (also available from the semester panel). Parents pay directly in the fictional game: **nothing leaves the player's wallet, bank or any real account**. Until the current semester's fee is marked paid, scheduled lectures do not record attendance; other activities remain open. A new bill appears each semester, including a repeated semester, with paid receipts saved locally.
- **Courses and academic district:** Computer Science, Medicine, Mass Communication, Business Administration, Law, Nursing, Pharmacy, Civil Engineering, Accounting, Economics, Architecture and Education. Each of the **12 departments** has its own building with **three enterable lecture halls (A, B and C)**, for 36 departmental halls in all, plus the existing general lecture hall. Department halls have teachers, classmates, notes and course-specific questions. Lectures in your own department (or the general hall) count toward attendance **only during the scheduled daily window**; visiting another department does not. Tap the always-visible 📚 lecture chip or **📚 Timetable** for Monday–Friday class times, subject and suggested hall. Classes missed outside that window are recorded as absences when you sleep; classrooms and other activities remain accessible. Prominent overhead building names and entrance plaques stay visible. Tap the mini-map (or 🗺 Map in the campus panel) to open the labelled interactive map; select a destination to see its gold walking route on both maps. These course titles and exam targets are fictional game content.
- **Online count:** a top-bar badge shows the number of connected players, including you, when using the shared Node server. It shows a dash when offline (including the standalone file). This counts connected guest sessions, not synchronized walking avatars.
- **Live fictional money transfers:** open **💬 Chat → 🌐 Guest chat** and select **Send ₦** beside an online player (or one of their current messages). Enter a whole-naira amount and confirm. The sender pays from the game **wallet only**; the recipient receives it instantly in their wallet, and both get a private notification. There is **no per-transfer cap** beyond the sender’s balance. Both must be connected to the same live server; offline/standalone play cannot send or receive money. Guest names are not used for addressing, so duplicate display names cannot redirect a transfer. No real money or bank details are involved.
- **Controls:** the game uses default WASD movement, arrows as alternatives, E or Space to interact, F to pass in football, and the interact key to shoot. Keyboard remapping and its setup prompts have been removed, including for returning saves; progress remains intact. On phones, tap **🕹 Use joystick / ✥ Use direction pad** to switch between the camera-relative analogue joystick on the **right** and direction buttons on the **left**; **Interact stays centred at the bottom** in both modes. The touch choice is saved. Drag or swipe the 3D view to rotate the camera.
- **Soundtrack & look:** the redesigned intro plays a short original mellow instrumental after the first tap or key press (browsers do not allow reliable sound before interaction). The **Campus** tab offers Calm Study, Quiet Courtyard and Evening Glow plus two louder original instrumental tracks, **Campus Pulse** and **Night Beat**, along with Music off, volume and mute. The welcome motif has also been made louder. Audio is synthesized locally with Web Audio, needs no external file or network, and its preferences save in the browser. Ambient pedestrians and several traders have Igbo names; Zara, Kunle and Timi keep their identities and relationship saves. Additional advertising billboards appear inside campus and along outside lodge streets.
- Visit the football pitch for a five-kick penalty challenge, a solo 45-second 4-vs-4 match with three chosen classmates, or a **live cooperative football rally**. In a live rally, two to four visitors take turns passing and choosing a goal lane against a server-controlled keeper. At least two visitors must join to start; up to four can participate.
- Visit the **library** and use the shared-study marker for a live **study circle**: two to four visitors contribute notes together. The solo library study session still works offline.
- A full semester lasts **20 school days**. Attend lectures, submit assignments and study; from semester day 16, sit the course-specific three-question final exam. Attendance, coursework and exam marks contribute 40%, 25% and 35%. At least 50/100 **and an exam attempt** are required to pass; otherwise the semester repeats. A transcript and cumulative five-point GPA appear under **🎓 Semester**. Eight passed semesters complete the fictional degree. The in-game clock drives sunrise, sunset, nighttime sky and moon, glowing windows and streetlamps, and warmer dusk lighting. Day–night changes are visual; non-lecture activities have no nighttime lockouts. Lectures are the exception and require attending on time. Sleeping starts the next school day.
- Visit **Residence Walk** on campus for four three-story halls: Maple, Cedar, Unity and Summit. Rents per 30 school days are fictional ₦9,000, ₦11,000, ₦13,000 and ₦15,000. Choose a floor (1–3) and room (A/B); walk through the lobby, take the stair markers and open your own room. Campus rooms include a bed and bathroom, but **do not have a furnishing activity**. Outside three-story lodges remain rentable and furnishable with a desk, rug, plant or lamp. You can bathe and sleep in your paid room. Rent is charged every **30 school days**; insufficient funds trigger a three-day grace period. Existing active 20-day leases are extended by 10 school days once, without a new charge. Semester length and the family’s separate 20-day stipend cycle stay unchanged.
- **Interactive map:** click or tap the compact mini-map (or use **🗺 Map** in the campus panel). Zoom with the controls or mouse wheel, drag to pan, search or filter the full building list, and tap a building on the map or in the list to set a saved walking waypoint. A gold route through walkable corridors and the gate appears on both maps; it updates as you travel. **Clear waypoint** removes it. The map freezes movement while open. It marks your location at the building entrance when you are indoors.
- **Walking stamina and traffic:** walking spends exactly **10 Energy per kilometre** (0.01 per metre actually travelled), per the clarified request. This is much less drain than the old 320 points/km; at zero Energy you still move, but at 42% normal speed. Water, juice, food and sleep help restore Energy. NPC vehicles, passenger rides and player-driven vehicles briefly stop after collisions and resume on the same heading. Only a player involved pays **₦100**; collisions cause **no Energy loss**. NPC-to-NPC bumps carry no player charge. A short cooldown prevents rapid repeat charges.
- **Traders:** each of the five roadside traders has two distinct specialties (breakfast, grilled food, fruit, hot meals or quick bites). Every stall also sells **water for ₦100** and **juice for ₦200**; drinks restore Energy without counting as a meal.
- Meet Zara, Kunle and Timi for repeatable day-by-day friendship scenes. Their friendship levels persist. Other students still support typed scripted NPC conversations.
- Campus shop, library, cafe, music gig and football-coaching jobs are now short **three-step playable tasks**, with base wages and accuracy bonuses. Only one paid shift is allowed per day. The weekly class-attendance allowance is ₦500 per attended lecture, plus a ₦1,000 perfect-attendance bonus; it pays once after the fifth day. A fictional family stipend is available once per 20-day month. Your course outfit is issued for free on starting university; boutique purchases go into a bag.

## Prototype boundaries

- Guest chat, live football, study rooms and wallet transfers use a single in-memory server. They have **no accounts, persistent matchmaking, synchronized walking avatars, or reliable anti-cheat**. Transfers are private, fictional and session-only; both guests must stay connected. The server validates connected recipients, whole-naira amounts, available session wallet balances and replay IDs, but browser wallet saves are editable and delivery or recovery across disconnections is **not guaranteed**. These are not secure or real-money payments. Server restarts clear live sessions; progress and furnished rooms save **locally in each browser**, not to a shared account. The standalone file falls back to solo play and NPC chat without live transfers.
- The required mini JAMB chapter, its score targets, university, courses, grades, prices, bursary and scholarships are fictional and are not official admissions guidance. NPC students are treated as having already completed admission. NPC replies are scripted, not live AI. Real-money payments are **not available**, and no payment details are collected.
- Public chat has a basic word/link filter, rate limits, mute and reports but **no authentication or robust moderation**. Do not deploy publicly without serious safety, privacy, account, abuse-prevention and operations work.
- To edit the game, change `app.js`, `template.html` or `server.cjs` and rebuild with `npm run build`. `build.mjs` embeds the client bundle into standalone `index.html`; no external CDN is needed.
